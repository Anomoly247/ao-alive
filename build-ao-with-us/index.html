AO Founding Supporter Sprint · Anom Originals

This is the proposed supporter campaign for the AO Universe — its supporter paths, its milestone, its launch gates, and the creator-rights boundaries that protect everyone who builds here. Read it before anything opens.

Current status: Draft-only

The campaign is currently a draft-only concept. No tiers are verified, no payments are active, no assets are approved, and no outreach is authorized. Gate 0 is not yet passed.

⬡ The decision in front of us

We are not approving the launch today. We are approving the requirements that must be met before any launch is even considered.

Progress has to move from creative drafting to a rigid, evidence-led approval workflow. Four gates, four different proofs: readiness review, test & preview, and launch — each one earned, none of them skipped.

⬡ The milestone

Gate 0 milestone · use-of-funds
$5,000

The sprint’s milestone is a $5,000 use-of-funds plan, approved by the founder — one of the Gate 0 readiness items that must be in place before the campaign can move forward.

⬡ Supporter paths — what support means here

The sprint defines the path support would travel — and the lines it never crosses. Exact tier names, prices, benefits, and refunds must be documented and frozen at Gate 0 before any supporter path opens. Nothing is for sale yet.

🎁

Digital benefits only

Supporter offers are documented as digital benefits — with zero ambiguity about what a supporter receives. The offer register at Gate 1 must prove that clarity before anything opens.

🚫

Never ownership. Never investment.

Support is not ownership and not an investment. Using “ownership” or “investment” language without legal review is an automatic failure at Gate 0 — and strict non-investment copy is one of the campaign’s critical safeguards.

🛡️

Adults buy. The art stays family-safe.

Purchasing language is strictly adult-only. The creative art itself stays SFW — that boundary is checked word by word in the Gate 1 copy review.

⬡ The five launch gates

No gate, no launch. Each gate has pass standards, required evidence, and stop conditions — and any change after approval requires a new review.

0 Readiness

Gate 0 — Internal readiness

Internal verification of domains, offers, assets, and the IP rights ledger.

  • Domains: primary & checkout verified and spelled correctly
  • Milestone: $5,000 use-of-funds approved by founder
  • Offers: tier names, prices, benefits, and refunds documented
  • Assets: IP inventory and permission records created
  • Rights: contributor ownership and credit terms ledger established

Automatic failures:

  • “Ownership” or “investment” language without legal review
  • Public assets with unknown ownership or permission status
  • Exposure of unreleased prompts or private AI conversations
  • Implied rights transfer through unsolicited submissions
1 Readiness review

Gate 1 — The first gate to pass

A local, internal audit of the landing page, checkout flow, and adult-only boundaries. Every criterion below needs its required evidence:

  • Content accuracy — claims match verified proof points; planned features clearly labeled
  • Offer & rights clarity — zero ambiguity on digital benefits vs. non-ownership / non-investment
  • Adult boundary — purchasing language strictly adult-only; creative art stays SFW
  • Fulfillment integrity — a named owner can deliver every promised asset within the stated time
  • Privacy & security — no secrets, admin links, or unreleased prompts in page source
2 Test mode

Gate 2 — Test-mode commerce

Prove that checkout, webhooks, fulfillment, and refunds behave correctly — in test mode first. No asset may be delivered solely because a success screen appeared.

Stop if: webhook fails, asset exposure before payment, or redirect loop.
3 Preview

Gate 3 — Small adult preview

Test the campaign with a narrowly defined adult opt-in preview audience. Monitor fulfillment reliability and rights questions the whole way through.

Stop if: ownership dispute, unsafe asset discovery, or accidental child-targeting.
4 Launch approval

Gate 4 — Public launch approval

Public release only after final approval of every domain of the campaign:

  • Identities — campaign name, brand version, owner assignment
  • Domains — primary URL, checkout URL, redirect rules
  • Commerce — exact price & currency, Stripe mode, tax configuration
  • Assets — IP rights inventory, fulfillment files, attribution ledger
  • Copy — landing page, email sequence, social outreach
  • Fulfillment — webhook events, delivery window, rollback owner
No “close enough” substitutions permitted after approval. Any change requires new review.

⬡ Creator-rights boundaries

These rules protect every creator whose work touches AO — and they are part of the campaign itself, not fine print after it.

✋

No implied transfer

Creators retain ownership of their original work — characters, art, writing, code — unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.

Submission is not permission.

📝

Specific permission

Permission must be documented for every asset. Availability is not permission to copy, scrape, remix, train on, or redistribute AO creative assets.

Credit is not ownership.

🌈

SFW protections

Public-safe does not mean public-domain. Unresolved assets are ineligible for publication, sale, or product inclusion.

Public-safe ≠ public-domain.

⬡ IP & brand safeguarding

The risks the sprint names out loud — and the safeguard standing against each one.

Appropriation by larger entities Critical risk. Safeguard: dated source evidence, asset inventory, IP notice.
Mistaken ownership interpretation Critical risk. Safeguard: strict non-investment copy, counsel review.
Contributor ownership dispute High risk. Safeguard: rights ledger, specific permissions, written terms.
Unreleased asset exposure High risk. Safeguard: access controls, redaction review, secure storage.
Brand / trademark confusion Medium risk. Safeguard: consistent identifiers, clearance check, takedown inbox.
